# Journal Figure Specifications

Exact physical width requirements for leading peer-reviewed publications:

### 1. Nature & Nature Portfolio
- **1 column:** 89 mm (3.5 in)
- **1.5 column:** 120–136 mm (4.7–5.35 in)
- **2 column (full page):** 180 mm (7.08 in)
- **Max figure height:** 225 mm (8.85 in)
- **Fonts:** Arial, Helvetica, or San-Serif; 5–7 pt at final size. Panel letters: 8 pt bold.

### 2. Cell Press (Cell, Molecular Cell, Cancer Cell)
- **1 column:** 85 mm (3.35 in)
- **1.5 column:** 114 mm (4.49 in)
- **2 column:** 174 mm (6.85 in)
- **Max figure height:** 230 mm

### 3. Science (AAAS)
- **1 column:** 55 mm (2.16 in)
- **2 column:** 120 mm (4.72 in)
- **3 column (full page):** 175 mm (6.89 in)
- **Max figure height:** 240 mm
