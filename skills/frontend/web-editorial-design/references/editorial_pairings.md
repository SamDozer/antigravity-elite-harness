# Curated Editorial Typography Pairings

Break free from the generic Space Grotesk + Inter mono-culture. Use these expressive combinations:

### 1. Literary Contemporary (High Editorial Prestige)
- **Display / Titles:** `Newsreader` (Optical size 72, Italic flourishes for key phrases)
- **Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Optical size 14, 400 weight, 1.65 line height)
- **Accents / Micro-Labels:** `JetBrains Mono` (Uppercase, letter-spacing: 0.12em)

### 2. Architectural Avant-Garde (Tech & Scientific Publications)
- **Display / Titles:** `Fraunces` (Soft serif with distinctive terminals, variable font)
- **Body:** `General Sans` or `Geist` (Crisp geometric humanist)
- **Code:** `Fira Code`

### 3. Bold Editorial Poster (Product Launches & Portfolios)
- **Display:** `Syne` (Extra-bold 800, tight negative tracking `-0.03em`)
- **Body:** `Cabinet Grotesk` or `Work Sans` (Regular 400, relaxed tracking)
